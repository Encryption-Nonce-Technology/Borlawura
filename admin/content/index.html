FAQs

  • How fast is a Borla Wura pickup?

    Most pickups in Accra are matched in under 3 minutes and collected within 20 minutes.

  • Which payment methods are supported?

    MTN MoMo, Telecel Cash and AirtelTigo Money. Cash is not accepted.

  • What is a community pickup code?

    Neighbours sharing one code split a single trip and each save 15%.

  • Are collectors verified?

    Every collector passes Ghana Card, DVLA licence and vehicle checks before going live.
