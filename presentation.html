MARKET CONTEXT & CHALLENGE

Transforming Urban Waste Management in Ghana

Overcoming municipal collection gaps, uncontrolled burning, and informal sector friction

CURRENT BOTTLENECKS

Urban Waste Failure Points

  • ✕
    Irregular Municipal Rounds: Households wait weeks for trash trucks, resulting in roadside piles and gutter blockages.
  • ✕
    Arbitrary Cash Pricing: Informal "aboboyaa" tricycle operators charge fluctuating, opaque prices without receipts.
  • ✕
    Toxic Open Air Burning: Frustrated residents burn mixed plastics in neighborhood backyards, emitting carcinogens.
  • ✕
    Wasted Hauler Mileage: Collectors spend up to 40% of their workday roaming streets looking for jobs.
